# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Stack
Web: zero-dependency installable PWA. Native Android/iOS: Expo Router and React Native components with react-native-svg, offline-bundled scenes, and local AsyncStorage. All three clients share the same original toy mechanics.

## Users
Children approximately ages 2–8 exploring open-ended educational toys, with adults managing the device and local settings.

## Product Purpose
Poklike is a free, public, independently drawn digital playroom inspired by the *mechanics* of exploratory Montessori-style toy collections. Every available toy should start without registration, payment, advertising, or a network connection after the first full load.

## Operating Context
Touch-first iPad, iPhone, Android tablet/phone and desktop browsers. Portrait and landscape supported, with landscape providing larger targets on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 25 named toy categories as of the source catalogue on 2026-10-10.
- Original code, art and generated audio only; do not redistribute Pok Pok's assets, copy, proprietary wording or brand.
- Offline only after the full initial download; visible ready confirmation, fail closed on cache failure; keep previous cache if an update fails.
- No server, accounts, analytics, subscriptions or ads. Local saved play and an adult-only preferences area.
- Semantic atomic commits and semantic pull request titles.
- Public documentation/recordings are sources of observed behaviour, not authority for unobservable details. Further first-party user recordings are requested for high-confidence parity review.

## Evidence on Hand
- Official catalogue at https://playpokpok.com/ (25 toys, high confidence for names and themes).
- Public App Store editorial: https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/story/id1894953131 (some specific observed behaviours).
- Public app description: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playpokpok.pokpok (core playroom UX, offline use).
- No user recordings, proprietary code, original game assets or installed app access supplied.

## Product Principles
- Open-ended discoveries, no scores, timers that punish, or artificial urgency.
- All local interactions with calm audio and motion, with reduction controls.
- No child data leaves the browser.
- Accessibility via keyboard actions, readable labels and large hit regions when geometry permits.
